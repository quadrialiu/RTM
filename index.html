How Live Pins works

  • Browse: dots on the map are events (purple) and side quests (orange). Tap one to see its details.
  • Join: open a pin and tap I'm in. If the host added a contact, use it to get in touch.
  • Add a pin: tap a spot on the map, then Add pin. Or tap + Add pin and search an address.
  • Events have a start and end time and are for formal occasions like paid parties, beach clean-ups, or blood drives. Side quests are casual plans, like trying a new restaurant, that disappear at a time you choose.
  • Pins disappear automatically when their time is up.
  • Suggestions or bugs? Message @skyy_notsky on Instagram.

Before you go

Live Pins only connects people. We don't check pins or the people behind them, and we're not responsible for meetups. Meet in public places, tell a friend where you're going, and don't share private details. Use your own judgment.
